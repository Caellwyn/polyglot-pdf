The real cost is the wait
A client often can't start a form until an interpreter is free—which can mean days or weeks.
Appointments get rescheduled. Care and legal work are delayed. Without proper interpretation, people face misdiagnoses, medication errors, and denied or delayed services. The shortage isn't a staffing line item—it's a barrier to care, status, and safety.
The product

Polyglot turns form-filling into a conversation.

A person speaks naturally in their own language. Polyglot explains each question, captures the answer, confirms it aloud, and assembles a clean, schema-compliant PDF—accurate enough to file.

Detects missing or illogical answers and asks again—so submissions are complete.

05
