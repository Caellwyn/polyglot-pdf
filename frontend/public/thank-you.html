📝 What just happened?

If this were a real form, it would have been submitted to the clinic's system. However, this is a demonstration environment designed to showcase the capabilities of voice-powered form filling.

The Voice Form Filler technology uses Google's Gemini Live API to enable natural conversation while filling out complex forms — making healthcare intake faster and more accessible.

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

All form data entered during this demo has been deleted and was never stored on any server. We do not retain, analyze, or share any field values you provided.
