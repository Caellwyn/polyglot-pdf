🎤 Voice Form Filler

Fill web forms with your voice - One-click installation

✨ What is this?

Voice Form Filler is a bookmarklet that adds voice-controlled form filling to any website. Simply speak, and watch the fields fill automatically!

📌 Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

🎤 Voice Form Filler

Chrome users: Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar

📖 Installation Instructions

1

Show your bookmarks bar

Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) in Chrome to show the bookmarks bar if it's hidden.

2

Drag the button

Click and drag the 🎤 Voice Form Filler button above to your bookmarks bar. Release to install.

3

Navigate to any form

Go to any website with a form (job application, contact form, survey, etc.)

4

Click the bookmark

Click the "Voice Form Filler" bookmark in your bookmarks bar. A purple overlay will appear.

5

Connect and speak!

Click "Connect to Voice" in the overlay, allow microphone access, and start speaking. The AI will fill the form fields for you!

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Requires Chrome 114+ with microphone access
  • Currently loads from localhost (dev mode)
  • For production, the bundle will be hosted on Google Cloud Storage
  • Works on most websites (some may block due to CSP security policies)
