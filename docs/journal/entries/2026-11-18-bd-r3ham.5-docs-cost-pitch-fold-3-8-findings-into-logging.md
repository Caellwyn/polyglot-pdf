## Trigger

This is phase 6 of epic `polyglot-pdf-r3ham`, the write-up. Phases 1-4 measured `gemini-3.8-live` against 2.5 (PRs #913 and #914). The investor material in `pitch/` still quoted only 2.5 numbers. The plan marks pitch numbers as consequence-bearing, so this PR is the maintainer's merge.

## What shipped

- **`pitch/i90-stress-test/README.md`** gets a dated 3.8 section, and a pointer at the top tells readers not to quote the 2.5 figures as current. It says:
  - There is no measured 3.8 I-90 yet: all three runs derailed (bd r3ham.10, r3ham.16).
  - On a like-for-like W-7, 3.8 costs about 30% more than 2.5.
  - About $3.2 per I-90 is an EXTRAPOLATION and is labelled as one.
  - Latency stays LOCAL.
  - The lite helper model (PR #912) has no regression.
  - Deployed telemetry (PR #908) is one staging session, too few to quote.
- **`pitch/naive-vs-mitigated-cost/README.md`** gets a dated repricing section. The same 2.5 conversation at 3.8 prices is $3.05 mitigated vs $27.17 naive, so the reduction grows from 7.1x to 8.9x. The section explains why a measured 3.8 run would cost more than a pure repricing.
- **`docs/standards/ai-models.md`**: 3.8 always thinks (about 3%, an upper bound). BLOCKING has a known cost: the barge-in wedge, bd dakii.
- The `logging-and-cost.md` invariant and the CLAUDE.md line had already landed with e72on in PR #914.

## Lessons

- **A repricing is not a re-measurement.** Holding token counts fixed and swapping rates explains about three quarters of 3.8's measured +30% on W-7 (about +23%). The rest is model behavior: twice the spoken audio, and thinking. Investor figures should say which one they are.
