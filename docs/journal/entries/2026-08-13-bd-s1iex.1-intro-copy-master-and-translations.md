## Lessons

- **Chirp3 run-to-run DURATION variance is ~9% at clip length, not "a few
  percent."** Identical text measured 81.6/81.6/88.6s. A single measurement at a
  window boundary is meaningless; freeze copy centered with margin for the draw,
  and remember production ships ONE cached draw from that distribution. (Added a
  one-liner to `docs/standards/testing.md` citing this entry.)
- **A measured gain does not transfer across content length.** +4 dB was
  correct for 4-second spell-back clips (peak 0.975, bd 9oejl) and CLIPS a 90s
  clip (raw-waveform peak 1.000). Gain is per-voice AND per-use-case.
- **Cloud TTS has no mid-tier encode for Chirp3 voices**: compressed output is
  ~32 kbps take-it-or-leave-it; only LINEAR16 is high fidelity. Any future
  "better audio" ask means server-side transcoding, not a request parameter.
- The enumerate-the-set discipline paid for itself in data: deriving the test's
  language set from `SPOKEN_LANGUAGE_BCP47` (not the 18 names in the file)
  turned "someone adds language 19 without legal copy" from a review-round
  hazard into a CI failure.
