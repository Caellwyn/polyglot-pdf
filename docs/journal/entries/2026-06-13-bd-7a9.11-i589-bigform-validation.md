## Trigger

`bd polyglot-pdf-7a9.11`, the I-589 follow-up under the PDF->JSON authoring epic
`7a9` (sibling to 7a9.10). 7a9.10 validated the LLM-native architecture on the
SMALL form (I-90, F1 ~97); this bead stress-tests it on the BIG, table-heavy,
relationship-heavy form (I-589: 460 widgets, 12 pages, 9 tables) -- the case the
7a9.9 prototype never covered. The two open questions: Q5 (does the enriched
bundle help the 9-table consolidation?) and Q6 (does the 460-widget single-shot
complete, where gemini-3.1-pro timed out in the bake-off, or is chunking needed?).

## What shipped

Three scored I-589 arms + a chunked-pass prototype + a findings doc. Throwaway
research output (drafts gitignored).

**Q6 -- the single-shot completes.** Opus produced a full schema from the
460-widget form in one call (~28 min, 168K output tokens), no timeout, no
truncation -- the 7a9.10 file-delivery fix carries the large output. **No chunking
is required for completion.** (gemini-3.1-pro timed out on the SMALLER hybrid
prompt in the bake-off; the Opus-via-CLI path does not.)

**Q5 -- the enriched bundle does NOT solve consolidation -- and REGRESSES vs the
bake-off.** All LLM-native arms detect 0/9 tables, but the committed bake-off's
**claude-hybrid got 7/9 tables and b.cond 41.5%** at the SAME F1 (29.8):

| arm | fields (gold 156) | F1 | tables | b.cond |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| llm-native single-shot | 433 | 28.9 | **0/9** | 0.0 |
| llm-native few-shot | 349 | 31.3 | **0/9** | ~1 |
| llm-native chunked | 402 | 31.9 | **0/9** | ~2 |
| bake-off claude-hybrid | 408 | 29.8 | **7/9** | **41.5** |
| bake-off claude-multimodal | 143 | 2.0 | **8/9** | 1.5 |

So the enrichment layer did not just fail to HELP big-form tables -- it HURT them
vs the plainer claude-hybrid prompt. Likely mechanism: the enriched
one-line-per-widget field list primes one-field-per-widget output, fighting the
table/option consolidation claude-hybrid's plainer prompt left the model free to
do. The gold collapses ~20 flat widgets (a 4-col x N-row table) into one
`columns`/`multi_entry` table field; I-90 has 0 tables (-> llm-native scored ~97),
I-589 has 9 (-> 0/9). Tables + b.cond are the discriminating metrics, and on both
the enriched bundle is worse than the existing claude-hybrid.

**Chunking hypothesis FALSIFIED.** The obvious theory was context overload (the
model loses the consolidation thread across 460 widgets). I built
`arm_llm_native_chunked.py` to test it -- per-page extraction (each page at I-90
scale) then merge. It did NOT help: 402 fields, still 0/9 tables; the model
under-consolidates a 59-widget page just as it does the whole form (page 3: 99
widgets -> 99 fields, a 1.00 ratio). So context size is not the cause, and chunking
additionally broke global structure (40 sections vs the gold's 9, since no chunk
sees the whole form). The 7a9.12 recommendation is NOT chunking and NOT
necessarily a new table pass: **start from claude-hybrid's plainer prompt (which
already gets 7/9 tables) for big forms, and reserve the enriched bundle for
small/flat forms** -- since the enrichment is what appears to suppress
consolidation. A dedicated table detector (the 7a9.10 annotated-page probe) is the
fallback. Input to 7a9.12.

Full numbers, the why, and the recommendation:
`docs/research/2026-06-13-7a9.11-i589-bigform-validation-findings.md`.

## Lessons

- **Test the obvious hypothesis instead of asserting it.** "The big form fails
  because it is too big for one shot" was wrong -- per-page chunking (small chunks)
  failed identically. A 20-minute chunk control falsified the context-overload
  story and relocated the real cause (table semantics), which changes the 7a9.12
  recommendation (add a table pass, don't chunk).
- **A 0% (b) score can be an artifact of a failed (a) layer.** The model DID emit
  37 show_if; they scored 0 because the scorer gates conditional-credit on field
  matching, and the diverged consolidation means the relationships reference field
  ids that do not line up. Don't read a downstream 0% as "the model can't do (b)";
  trace whether the upstream layer it depends on collapsed first.
- **The architecture's ceiling is form-shape-dependent, not just model-dependent.**
  Same engine, same prompt: ~97 on a flat form, ~30 on a table-heavy one. The
  residual epic hours live in the table/multi-entry structure, exactly where the
  original epic framing predicted -- now localized to one addressable pass.
