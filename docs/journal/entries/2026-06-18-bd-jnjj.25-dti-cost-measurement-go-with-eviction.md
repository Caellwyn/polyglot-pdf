**The result -- GO on DTI + calibrated eviction:**

| arm | happy | unhappy |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (full-SI section-reset) | $0.2219 | $0.5596 |
| DTI alone | $0.2378 (+7%) | $0.5490 (-2%) |
| **DTI + eviction** | $0.2247 (parity) | **$0.4407 (-21%)** |

DTI's SI is 34% smaller (5718->3786 chars), but DTI ALONE is a wash -- the
per-field guidance rides the confirm_field response and ACCUMULATES within a
section, re-billed every turn, giving the saving back. DTI + a TIGHT compression
window (trigger 2800 / window 2200, just above the ~1978-tok unevictable SI+tools
floor) purges that accumulation turn-over-turn and wins -21% on the costlier
unhappy path. Empirically confirms the plan's Gate-1.5 hypothesis end-to-end.

## Lessons

- **A smaller SI is necessary but not sufficient -- accumulation is the hidden
  cost.** DTI shrank the per-turn SI 34% and still washed, because what you move
  OUT of the static SI into the conversation gets re-billed every turn as it
  accumulates. The cost lever is "tiny SI AND bounded accumulation," and the only
  thing that bounds accumulation here is eviction.
- **Gemini compression is weaker than its window suggests (l3a, again).** A loose
  window (2500) barely evicted (60-640 tok drops) and did not help; only a tight
  window just above the unevictable floor cut deep enough to win. Eviction
  calibration is load-bearing and form-specific.
- **A by-reference `from x import get_settings` defeats a module-attribute
  monkeypatch.** The harness's settings override silently missed ai_context (the SI
  builder), so the "DTI" arm rendered JIT/legacy until fixed. When patching a
  function for a test/spike, patch it on EVERY module that imported it by name, not
  just the defining module.
- **Always state the denominator on a cost number.** 25q is ~19% of the I-90; the
  ratio generalizes but the dollars do not. Caught by the maintainer; now explicit.
