## Trigger

Fourth Lane-2 bead of the schema-v2 epic `polyglot-pdf-h76m` (plan:
`~/.claude/plans/rustling-discovering-hearth.md`, P5.1). The W-7 failure that
triggered the epic was at root a GROUPING failure: the extractor had to
consolidate 8 sibling checkboxes "by meaning" with zero structural help and
collapsed their identity instead. The dump has every signal needed to SUGGEST
the family deterministically; nothing surfaced it to the model.

## What shipped

- `scripts/bakeoff/group_candidates.py`: mines the acroform dump for widget
  families that look like one logical question. Two family shapes:
  same-leaf-multi-index (radio kids; w9's `c1_1[0..6]`, w7's `c1_11[0..3]`)
  and consecutive numbered-leaf runs sharing page + column + on-value (w7's
  `c1_2..c1_9` reason boxes). Per-candidate signals reported
  (shared/distinct on-values, geometry alignment, tooltip prefix); reuses
  `structural_enrich.decompose_name`. `render_candidates_block` presents
  them as SUGGESTIONS with an adopt-as-one-field-or-split instruction and a
  never-drop-a-widget rule.
- Flag-gated end to end, default OFF: `arm_llm_native.build_prompt(
  group_candidates=)` (works in both plain and enriched prompt modes) ->
  `extract_form --group-candidates` -> `eval_extraction --extract
  --group-candidates` (the ablation arm); recorded in `_bakeoff` provenance.
  The user-facing switch-on belongs to P5.3 per the plan.
- Tests (tests/test_group_candidates.py): W-7 candidates contain
  `c1_2[0]..c1_9[0]` EXACTLY with no neighbor absorbed (exit criterion 1);
  W-9's classification family is one candidate with the lone 3b box
  excluded; prompt-block wording and flag gating pinned.

## The ablation (exit criterion 2)

Four real extractions (Vertex `gemini-2.5-pro`, ADC -- the same stack the
hosted extractor uses; the local claude CLI is 401-unauthenticated on this
machine, noted below), scored by `eval_extraction`; summary JSON archived at
[../research/2026-07-05-h76m12-group-candidates-ablation.json](../research/2026-07-05-h76m12-group-candidates-ablation.json),
both W-7 drafts archived as repair-fixture inputs
(`backend/tests/fixtures/w7_candidates_{on,off}_draft.json`).

| form | arm | verdict | lint E | conv% | choice% | fieldF1 | structural |
|------|-----|---------|--------|-------|---------|---------|------------|
| w9-heldout | off | OK | 0 | 100 | 100 | - | PASS (8w, split) |
| w9-heldout | on  | OK | 0 | 100 | 100 | - | PASS (8w, split) |
| w7 | off | RED | 0 | 100 | 100 | 99.2 | FAIL (8 problems) |
| w7 | on  | RED | 5 | 0   | 100 | 93.9 | FAIL (1 problem) |

**Held-out (w9): identical across arms -- no metric regresses with the block
on.** Criterion 2 met.

**The w7 movement is the epic's thesis in one table.** OFF: the model split
the group into 8 standalone checkboxes (today's-gold style) whose single
options all lack widget mapping -- 8 structural problems, unfixable by a
repair pass (nothing ties option to widget). ON: the model ADOPTED the
candidate as ONE field with EVERY option carrying its own correct
pdf_field_name -- the grouping/identity problem solved; what remains is pure
VOCABULARY: every option.value is the PDF's shared on-value "1" (the
duplicate-value lint error) and the composite conditions use an invented
`or` operator (4 lint errors). Those are exactly the defects P1.2's
contract-from-model and P5.2's repair own downstream -- and they are now
REPAIRABLE (slugs mint from labels; the widget mapping the repair needs is
present) and VISIBLE (lint catches both; the two-gate seam keeps such a
draft un-promotable). The conv% 0 reads against the v1 widget-leaf value
convention, which the v2 slug world deliberately replaces.

The flag therefore stays default OFF until P5.2's repair lands; the on-arm
draft is committed as the repair pass's primary test input.

## Lessons

- A structural hint changes WHAT KIND of failure the model produces:
  without candidates it fails at grouping (unrepairable -- information
  lost); with candidates it fails at vocabulary (repairable -- identity
  preserved). Moving failures downstream into deterministic-fixable
  territory is a win even when headline metrics look mixed.
- The local claude CLI is 401-unauthenticated in this environment (stored
  OAuth invalid; `claude login` is interactive and unavailable here) --
  extraction work in agent sessions goes through Vertex ADC, which also
  matches the production extractor.
