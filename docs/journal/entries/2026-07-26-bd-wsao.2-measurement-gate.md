# wsao.2 measurement gate: inline details works -- the constraint is the compression window (bd wsao.2)

## What happened

The sprint-16 step-2 gate ran overnight: a with/without-details A/B on the w7
reason-for-applying field through the real voice harness (Vertex
2.5-flash-native-audio, manual VAD), before the enrichment engine gets built.
Findings doc: `scripts/spikes/wsao2_inline_details_findings.md`; raw runs:
`scripts/spikes/data/wsao2_runs.json`.

## Headline results

- **Inline details delivers the intended value**: baseline answers the treaty
  deep question with "I can't answer it based on the fields I have"; the
  details arms answer correctly from the verbatim excerpt (Exception 1, no
  tax return required), concisely, with no read-aloud flooding -- and in the
  Spanish arm, fully in Spanish from ENGLISH reference text (no language-lock
  contamination).
- **Tool-calling never degraded** in any healthy run (correct field_id +
  value, ~500ms utterance-end-to-tool-call, confirm intact).
- **The real constraint is the ctu8 compression window, not attention**: a
  3,366-token details block breaches the derived ~6,400-token assembled
  budget -> Vertex closes 1007 at session ASSEMBLE (never opens). 1,717
  tokens fits. The per-field inline budget is form-dependent and computable
  from `derive_compression_window`.
- **The delegate-fallback is words, not deeds**: baseline SAID "I'm looking
  that up" but made zero ask_secretary calls -- the whole-booklet floor is
  unreachable until the phase-2 hook lands.

## Verdict recorded on the bead

Inline confirmed as the complex-field default UNDER A SIZE BUDGET; the
oversize policy (bake-time cap vs bigger window vs early phase-2) is the
maintainer's morning decision, exactly as the plan reserved it.

## Scar / notes

  tabs clamp setTimeout to ~1Hz (audio smear -> idle timeout, bd f58l
  re-hit), and a pure microtask burst overflows the backend audio queue
  (AUDIO_QUEUE_FULL drops). Channel pacing is real-time in any visibility
  state.
- Checkbox fields false-positive DOM assertions: `input.value` is always
  "on"; assert on sessionStorage ssValue/ssConfirmed.
- Seed BEFORE agree (the post-agree re-handshake computes the hint from
  client state at that instant); give the disclaimer session ~10s before
  clicking agree or the half-open Gemini session wedges.
- gen_fixtures.py now supports per-utterance gTTS language + skip-if-exists;
  five wsao.2 fixtures added (treaty question, box-a answer, Spanish
  variants).
